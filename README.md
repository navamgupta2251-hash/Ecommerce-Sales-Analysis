E-commerce Sales Analysis: Identifying Revenue Drivers & Operational Gaps
Overview

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data to understand what is driving performance and where inefficiencies exist.
The goal is to move beyond reporting metrics and instead identify patterns that can support better business decisions.

Business Problem

E-commerce businesses often face challenges such as fluctuating sales, high return rates, and delivery inefficiencies.
Without proper analysis, it becomes difficult to identify the root causes behind these issues.

This project aims to:

Identify factors affecting sales performance
Understand customer and regional behavior
Detect operational issues like returns and delivery delays
Provide actionable insights to improve overall performance
Key Business Questions
What factors are impacting overall sales performance?
Are returns and delivery delays affecting revenue?
Which regions or product categories are underperforming?
What patterns exist in customer purchasing behavior?
Where can the business improve efficiency and reduce losses?
Dataset Description

The dataset contains over 34,000+ e-commerce transactions, including:

Order details
Product categories
Sales and revenue data
Customer information
Delivery and return indicators
Analysis Approach

The analysis was performed using Power BI, SQL, and Power Query with a focus on extracting meaningful insights rather than just visualizing data.

Key Steps:
Data cleaning and transformation to handle inconsistencies
Data modeling to enable efficient analysis
Exploratory analysis to identify trends and anomalies
KPI tracking for sales, returns, and performance metrics
Analysis Performed
Sales trend analysis to identify growth and decline patterns
Regional performance comparison to detect underperforming areas
Return rate analysis to understand product-level issues
Delivery delay analysis to evaluate operational efficiency
Customer behavior analysis to identify spending patterns
Key Insights
Certain regions consistently underperform in terms of revenue
High return rates are concentrated in specific product categories
Delivery delays are linked to lower customer satisfaction and repeat purchases
A small group of products contributes to a significant portion of total revenue
Customer purchasing patterns vary significantly across regions
Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

Improve logistics in regions with frequent delivery delays
Investigate and reduce return rates for specific product categories
Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products and regions
Optimize inventory for products contributing the most revenue
Monitor customer behavior trends to improve retention strategies
Business Impact

This analysis helps:

Identify key areas of revenue leakage
Improve operational efficiency
Support data-driven decision-making
Highlight opportunities for growth and optimization
Tools & Technologies Used
Power BI – Data visualization and dashboard creation
SQL – Data querying and transformation
Excel / Power Query – Data cleaning and preprocessing
Dashboard & Visualization

The dashboard provides a clear view of:

Sales performance

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how data can be used not just to report performance, but to understand underlying issues and guide business decisions.
The focus is on identifying problems, analyzing causes, and suggesting practical solutions.
