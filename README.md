# 📊 E-commerce Sales Analysis: Identifying Revenue Drivers & Operational Gaps

---

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify drivers of performance and pinpoint inefficiencies.  
The goal is to move beyond reporting and support better business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem
E-commerce businesses face challenges like fluctuating sales, high returns, and delivery delays.  
Without proper analysis, identifying root causes becomes difficult.

### This project aims to:
- Identify factors affecting sales performance  
- Understand customer and regional behavior  
- Detect operational issues like returns and delays  
- Provide actionable insights  

---

## ❓ Key Business Questions
- What factors are impacting sales performance?  
- Are returns and delays affecting revenue?  
- Which regions or categories are underperforming?  
- What patterns exist in customer behavior?  
- Where can efficiency be improved?  

---

## 📂 Dataset Description
The dataset contains **34,000+ transactions**, including:
- Order details  
- Product categories  
- Sales and revenue  
- Customer information  
- Delivery & return indicators  

---

## ⚙️ Analysis Approach
Performed using **Power BI, SQL, and Power Query**:

- Data cleaning and transformation  
- Data modeling  
- Exploratory analysis  
- KPI tracking  

---

## 📈 Analysis Performed
- Sales trend analysis  
- Regional performance comparison  
- Return rate analysis  
- Delivery delay analysis  
- Customer behavior analysis  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Some regions underperform in revenue  
- High returns exist in specific categories  
- Delivery delays impact repeat purchases  
- Few products drive most revenue  

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Improve logistics in delayed regions  
- Reduce returns in specific categories  
- Focus marketing on high-performing products  
- Optimize inventory  

---

## 📊 Business Impact
- Identifies revenue leakage  
- Improves operational efficiency  
- Supports data-driven decision-making  

---

## 🛠 Tools Used
- Power BI  
- SQL  
- Excel / Power Query
